# Skyscrape glossary

Skyscrape helps internal analysts investigate land and single-family housing activity. These terms describe what an analyst can conclude from public records.

## Campaigns and coverage

**Campaign**. A saved research objective, geography, requested fields, source policy, schedule and budget. A permit-monitoring campaign collects evidence.

**Run**. One execution of a particular campaign version. A partial run can contain accepted observations and unresolved gaps.

**Qualified source**. A source approved for a particular use after checking publication rights, fees, coverage, metadata and retrieval limits. Qualification applies to the assessed jurisdiction and fields.

**Coverage gap**. A requested place, period or field for which eligible sources supply insufficient evidence. A gap leaves activity unknown.

## Records and evidence

**Observation**. A dated record of what one source published, with its original identity and values. A later correction creates a new revision.

**Provenance**. The source, record identity, event date when known, retrieval time, artifact reference and extraction version behind a field value.

**Field assertion**. A value attributed to a subject with supporting or conflicting observations. Its evidence state is verified, corroborated, inferred, conflicted, unknown or stale.

**Permit**. A jurisdiction's record of a regulated activity, identified by jurisdiction and permit number. Issuance alone establishes neither a construction start nor completion.

**Project**. A development activity that can involve several permits and parcels. A separate trade permit can describe work on the same project.

**Parcel**. A county-qualified unit of land with a dated assessor parcel number. Its boundaries and number can change.

**Ownership observation**. A source's dated identification of an owner. Assessor records and recorded instruments can disagree.

**Transfer**. An instrument documenting a change involving one or more parcels. A bulk consideration amount applies to the instrument unless evidence allocates it.

**Listing episode**. A source's dated offering of a property. An asking price and a recorded closing price describe different events.

**Buyer profile**. An entity's acquisition category, geography and criteria. Each criterion identifies whether the entity stated it, records demonstrate it or an analyst inferred it.

**Permit participant**. A party named in a permit with a specific role, such as applicant or contractor. Evidence of that role alone does not establish buying intent.

**Verified public business channel**. A published business contact linked by checked evidence to the organization and role, with a source and verification date. An uncertain personal detail is ineligible.

**Suppression**. A restriction on disclosing a contact, including one previously verified. It applies wherever the record is presented or exported.
